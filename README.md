# Sales Analysis | Amazon Products

## Overview
E-commerce businesses generate high-volume transactional and customer review data.  
This project analyzes Amazon product sales data to uncover **revenue trends, category performance, and product-level insights** using interactive Power BI dashboards.

The goal is to demonstrate the ability to:
- Translate sales data into strategic business insights
- Track KPI-driven performance metrics (YTD, QTD)
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Communicate findings through professional BI visualization

## Business Objective
- Measure overall Year-To-Date (YTD) and Quarter-To-Date (QTD) sales performance
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Analyze monthly and weekly sales trends
- Evaluate product performance using revenue and review metrics
- Support revenue optimization and category strategy decisions

---

## Key Analytical Insights
- **Men Shoes** category contributes the highest revenue share (~43% of total sales).
- Sales display strong seasonal patterns, particularly during Q4.
- Revenue concentration is skewed toward top-performing categories.
- Some products show high review engagement but moderate revenue, indicating potential marketing opportunities.
- Weekly sales spikes align with promotional or peak shopping periods.

---

## Business Recommendations
- **Prioritize High-Revenue Categories**  
  Expand inventory and promotional focus on Men Shoes and Camera segments.

- **Leverage High-Review Products**  
  Boost marketing for products with strong engagement but moderate revenue.

- **Seasonal Campaign Optimization**  
  Allocate higher marketing budgets during Q4 peak demand.

- **Introduce Profitability Metrics**  
  Future dashboards should integrate margin analysis for strategic pricing decisions.

- **Implement YoY & MoM Growth Tracking**  
  Add time-intelligence measures to track business growth trends.
